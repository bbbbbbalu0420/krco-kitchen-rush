# Features / Возможности

Kitchen Rush: Crazy Orders is in development. The screenshots show the actual game, with development-build overlays where present. No public game download is offered.

- 60 authored Career shifts, 20 Challenges and Free Play. Eight recipes; cooking, serving, dirty plates and washing.
- Serving trays, dish stacks and saved local progression. Cloud restaurant furniture and opt-in visibility are verified on staging.
- Dedicated co-op for 2–4 players is tested locally with separate Linux authority. Disconnect/rejoin, process-crash recovery, signed rewards and weekly ranking have automated evidence.
- Real opted-in district cards, read-only 3D interiors, walking, friends, private access, visit invitations, blocks and reports. Direct friend-to-dedicated-shift invitations are still incomplete.
- Portrait mobile controls exist; current-build physical iPhone acceptance is pending.

Игра разрабатывается. 60 сюжетных смен, 20 испытаний и свободная игра проверяются автоматической модельной матрицей. Физическое движение и сеть проверяются отдельными сценариями. Публичный сервер, релиз в магазинах и прямой переход из гостевого визита в dedicated-смену пока не готовы.
