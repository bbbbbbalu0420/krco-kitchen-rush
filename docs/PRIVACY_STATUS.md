# Privacy status / Статус приватности

This page describes development status, not a final privacy policy. No public game service is being offered through this repository.

The staging game uses Unity Authentication and Cloud services for account identity, private restaurant state, rewards and social permissions. Public restaurant listing is opt-in. Districts are virtual and do not use GPS. Read-only visits expose a limited interior snapshot; wallet and inventory fields are excluded. Reports and moderation data are restricted to private operational tools.

Account recovery and deletion have local/staging test evidence. Ranked accounts additionally require privileged score reconciliation before deletion can complete. Support mailbox, legal operator, retention periods, hosting region, provider agreements and final store declarations remain **OWNER_INPUT_REQUIRED**. These details have not been invented. Final RU/EN policies require owner review before launch.

Это описание текущей разработки, а не окончательная политика. Публикация ресторана добровольная; GPS не используется. Кошелёк и инвентарь не входят в гостевую выдачу. Контакты поддержки, оператор данных и сроки хранения ещё должны быть утверждены владельцем.
